Why devotees know this temple

Sacred significance

Chidambaram is revered in Shaiva tradition for Nataraja’s cosmic dance and for the temple’s association with Akasha—space or ether—within the Pancha Bhuta pilgrimage tradition. The complex also includes the Chit Sabha, Kanaka Sabha, Nritta Sabha, Deva Sabha and Raja Sabha.

Its religious landscape is broader than a single shrine: government and historical sources record associated shrines and spaces including Sivaganga, a Devi shrine, and Govindaraja Vishnu shrine.
